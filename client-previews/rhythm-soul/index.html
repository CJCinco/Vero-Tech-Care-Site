Competition Team

Our RSDC Team is for the dancer looking for additional performance opportunities and a more serious dedication to dancing. We attend two competition's a year and welcome all dancers beginning at the age of 5 years old. Our team is passion driven and requires an audition to be selected for a place on the team.

ABOUT US

Rhythm. Soul. Dance.

Rhythm & Soul was founded by Ashly Billé and opened in the fall of 2019. Our studio is a family friendly environment and welcomes all levels of dancers ages 2-18. We are striving to nurture well rounded dancers in and out of the studio by building confidence and self-esteem while having fun learning the art of dance. Our staff is kind, experienced, and hands on ready to help your child succeed. We value each student who walks through our doors, and every child is special. We can't wait to meet you!

CHILDREN'S DANCE

Something For Every Student

Rhythm & Soul's dance program offers classes from the ages 2.5 and up. We believe dance should be fun while working hard, exploring and creating. We strive to work with each student as individuals to gain confidence, learn technique, and help their passion for movement grow.

For our under 5 children, we offer Creative Movement classes as a building block to beginning dance instruction and a ballet and tap combo class where students can explore rhythm with their tap shoes. These classes are the foundation of movement before leveling up to the next class.

Our dance program also offers a end of the year recital where students showcase choreography that they learn in class beginning in January. Recital requires a costume purchase with additional fees to the monthly class price.
