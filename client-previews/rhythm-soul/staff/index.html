OUR STUDIO

Ashly Billé

Owner / Instructor

Ashly was born and raised in Westchester, NY and began dancing at the age of 3. She graduated from Roger Williams University in Rhode Island with a BA in Dance and a minor in Creative Writing.

Her professional dance career became a teaching career and ultimately led her to open Rhythm & Soul in fall 2019. She also holds RYT-200 yoga training plus certifications in Barre Above, group exercise, and SilverSneakers classes for seniors.

Maddie Mcintosh

Competition Director

Maddie has taught dance for seven years and has been teaching at Rhythm & Soul since it opened. Before teaching, she trained across many styles and competed throughout Florida, including winning Dance Worlds 2016.

She studied dance at IRSC and has taught and choreographed for studios across the Treasure Coast before finding her home at Rhythm & Soul.

Shelby Trembly

Competition Coordinator

Shelby has danced since age 3 and has performed in major event settings including bowl game halftime shows and the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.

She has an AA in Dance and a BS in Event Management and is excited to be part of Rhythm & Soul.
