FAQ

Questions people often ask before booking.

Do you offer in home and remote support?

Yes. In home service is available around Vero Beach and nearby homes. Remote sessions are available for many issues and are often the fastest option for quick fixes or follow up help.

What does 24/7 computer monitoring mean?

For covered computers, secure monitoring software can watch for important update, security, and device-health issues around the clock. It does not mean 24/7 emergency live support, and it does not include personal file browsing, passwords, banking access, or private account review.

Where do you provide in home tech support?

In home appointments are focused on Vero Beach, Indian River County, and nearby homes by appointment. Remote support is available beyond the local area when the issue is a good fit.

Can you help with passwords, security, and scams?

Yes. That includes simpler password systems, two factor login setup, basic security reviews, scam prevention settings, and account recovery planning. Help stays ethical and security focused.

What if something is urgent or hardware is broken?

Book the earliest available remote session online for the fastest path, or contact me directly if the situation is unusual. Hardware issues can be diagnosed, but internal component repair or specialized physical repair work is referred out when needed.
