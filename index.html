FAQ

Do you offer in-home and virtual support?

Yes. In-home service is available around Vero Beach and nearby homes. Remote sessions are available for many issues and are often the fastest option for quick fixes or follow-up help.

What if I am not sure which appointment to choose?

Choose remote for quick troubleshooting and follow-up help. Choose in-home for Wi-Fi, printers, setup, and multi-device issues. If your situation is unusual, more complex, or membership-related, call, text, or email before booking.

What kinds of devices do you help with?

Support includes Apple and Windows computers, iPhones, iPads, Android devices, printers, Wi-Fi equipment, smart TVs, streaming devices, and many everyday apps and accounts that connect them.

Can you help with passwords, security, and scams?

Yes. That includes simpler password systems, two-factor login setup, basic security reviews, scam-prevention settings, and account recovery planning. Help stays ethical and security-focused.

Can you set up a new phone, tablet, or computer?

Yes. New device setup can include accounts, updates, apps, syncing, transfers, email, printers, and making sure your important information ends up in the right place.

What if something is urgent and not working right now?

Book the earliest available remote session online for the fastest path. If you do not see a fit or the situation is unusual, contact directly and the best next step can be recommended.

Do you repair broken hardware?

Hardware issues can be diagnosed and the next step can be recommended, but board-level repair or specialized physical repair work is referred out when needed.
