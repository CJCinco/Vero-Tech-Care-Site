Before you decide

Back up your files

Back up your files as soon as it’s safe, using iCloud, another cloud service, or an external drive. After a spill, get clearance before powering on to back up.

Ask for one complete quote

Confirm parts, labor, diagnostics, tax, coverage and warranty. ACT says diagnostics are waived if you proceed with its recommended repair; the fee itself isn’t published.

Where the estimates come from

The symptom labels assume the keyboard is not working while the display, power and charging work. Colors indicate relative longer-term concern if damage is present, not the chance a part has failed. Board faults can affect overall reliability; a localized keyboard fault is a narrower concern. Working screen/charging reports do not establish safety. Conditional repair methods are not ranked. All repair-table numbers are rounded to the nearest $10, before tax and extra fees.

ACT Computers, Vero Beach publishes a $99 standard hourly rate. Its terms are dated June 14, 2023 and prices may change. No exact-model fixed repair price or diagnostic amount was found. Confirm the current rate, minimum charge and model support.

Deviceland lists parts for broad 13-inch Air categories: keyboard/top case $229, battery $159.99, USB-C/M1 display $399, and M2/M3 display $467. Labor is extra. These are not exact A-number quotes or ACT’s parts prices. The estimated hours here are 1.5–3 for a top case, 0.5–1.5 for a battery, and 1–2 for a display. Totals are parts plus those hours at $99.

For a common 2020 Intel Air in working condition, $200–$325 is a provisional comparison range, not an appraisal. Examples: sold i3 / 8 GB / 256 GB at $229.99; working i3 without charger at $189.99 + $19.99 shipping; Swappa October 5 averages of $314 for i3 / 256 GB, $291 for i5 / 256 GB and $316 for i5 / 512 GB. Those are different configurations, and sample sizes are unknown. Liquid history can lower the value. No reliable matching as-is price was established.

2018–2019 Air models use a butterfly keyboard; the 2020 Intel Air introduced the Magic Keyboard. Keyboard-only repairs can differ from a complete top case. Published examples from MacScreenRepair and MackTechs include $129–$379 keyboard-only, $400–$700 top case and $199 liquid cleaning. These are general references, not prices for a specific A2179. Don’t add overlapping keyboard and top-case work. Cleaning may not resolve all damage; logic-board work needs inspection and a quote. Data recovery references are $325–$600, separately from repair. Eligible accidental damage under active AppleCare may have a $299 fee plus tax; confirm eligibility.

Checked October 5, 2026. Quotes, taxes, shipping, battery condition and extra damage can change the comparison. No customer information is collected, stored or sent by this page.

Replacement budget ranges are rounded guides from selected older 16 GB refurbished offers to current Apple-new choices, not a market-wide minimum, maximum or typical-price distribution. The demanding group’s $1,999 new MacBook Pro uses the base M5 chip, not M5 Pro. Higher-memory or demanding GPU work may need a different configuration.

Connector/cable planning assumes 1–2 hours at ACT’s published $99/hour. A check/reseat may need no part; the upper figure allows an A1932 cable at $18.99 or an A2179/A2337 keyboard/trackpad cable at $14.99. These are retail parts references, not ACT’s prices. M2 shows check/reseat labor only because a keyboard-specific cable price was not verified. The hours are planning assumptions, not ACT estimates or billing minimums. Soldered connector damage, diagnostic fees, shipping, tax and markup can add cost.

The logic-board row uses Microsoldering.com’s published benchmark: $350 for 2018–2020 Intel Air and $500 for M-series Air. That provider lists these under no-power repairs, so they are comparison benchmarks, not quotes for a keyboard fault or ACT pricing. The actual repair can cost more or less and needs diagnosis.

For a 2020 Intel Air in the reported damaged condition, the $100–$200 estimated value is a rough judgment for planning, not a verified comparable-sales range or a purchase offer. It assumes the laptop otherwise works, with an external keyboard if needed, no other known faults, and its spill history disclosed. This is a conditional estimate, not a confirmed test of the laptop. Use after a spill still requires a safety assessment. The known working-value reference is $200–$325; repair scope, exact specs and buyer inspection can change the damaged value substantially. Other models require a separate assessment.
