FAQ

Questions business owners often ask.

Do I need a new website or a redesign?

That depends on what you already have. If the current site has a useful foundation, a focused redesign may be the simplest path. If the platform, structure, or ownership is holding the business back, a new build may make more sense. I review the fit before quoting the project.

What does the $1,500 starting price include?

It is the starting point for a complete, focused small-business website project. The final price depends on the number of pages, content needs, integrations, special features, and the condition of the current setup. You receive the full scope and quote before work begins.

Can you work with the website platform I already have?

Often, yes. I review the current platform, access, content, and business goals before recommending whether to improve what is there or move to a simpler setup. I will not force a platform change when the current one can do the job well.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

I can review how your hosting, domain, and website accounts are set up and help organize the project. Your accounts and billing stay in your business's name whenever the platform allows it.

What happens after the website launches?

You can manage the website yourself, request updates as needed, or ask for a client-specific ongoing care quote. Website upkeep and broader help with Google, reviews, social pages, or contact flow are available when they fit the business.

Will a new website guarantee more leads or higher rankings?

No website can honestly guarantee leads or search rankings. The project can give you a clearer, more credible foundation that is easier for customers and search engines to understand.
