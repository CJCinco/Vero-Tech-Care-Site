FAQ.

What kinds of business technology do you help with?

I can help with business technology, digital setup, websites, online presence, customer contact paths, troubleshooting, and focused improvement projects. The consultation helps determine what actually fits.

Do I need to know exactly what service I need?

No. Tell me what you want to improve or what is getting in the way. I will help you identify the simplest useful next step during the free consultation.

Can you work with my current systems and accounts?

Often, yes. I review the relevant tools, platforms, access, and goals before recommending whether to improve what is there or move to a simpler setup. Your accounts and billing stay in your business's name whenever possible. Please never send passwords by email, text, or form.

Do you take on one-time projects and ongoing support?

Yes. Some businesses need one focused improvement or troubleshooting project. Others benefit from continued care. We can decide what makes sense after the consultation, with a clear scope and quote before work begins.

Will you make changes without my approval?

No. I explain the recommendation, confirm the scope, and get your approval before making important changes to accounts, systems, or public content.

Can you help when several tools need to work together?

Yes. We can look at how your devices, software, accounts, customer contact paths, and online tools fit together, then choose the simplest useful place to start.
