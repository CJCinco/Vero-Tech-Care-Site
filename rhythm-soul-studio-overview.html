Rhythm & Soul Studio Overview

General System Strategy

Each studio will use a simple wired passive-speaker system: a pair of passive speakers connects directly to its amplifier/receiver, and the amplifier/receiver plugs into a standard outlet.

Passive speakers→Amplifier / receiver→Standard outlet

Parts Needed

  • Four passive speakers — two for each studio.
  • Speaker cable for all speaker-to-receiver runs, sized after the on-site measurements.
  • One additional amplifier/receiver for Studio C; Studio A’s existing receiver will be tested for reuse.
  • Mounting hardware, including rated brackets, appropriate fasteners, and required cable-management materials.

The rooms

Studio A — larger room

Mount two speakers high on the diagonal-accent wall, opposite the mirror. The existing Insignia receiver will be bench-tested for reuse. Two separate speaker-cable runs will use a high, paintable surface raceway, with all brackets anchored into verified backing.

Studio C — smaller room

Mount two speakers high on the clear wall above the fixed barres, opposite the mirror. A dedicated WiiM Amp will keep this room independent. Two separate speaker-cable runs will use a clean, paintable surface raceway, with brackets anchored into verified backing.
